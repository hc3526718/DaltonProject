COOKIE POLICY

Cookies & similar technologies

This policy explains how The Dalton Grant Academy (The Dalton Grant Academy platform) uses cookies and similar technologies on the website and in authenticated web sessions. Replace contact details before production launch.

1. What we use

  • Essential — session and security tokens (e.g. Supabase auth on web).
  • Preferences — accessibility and notification choices when stored in your account.
  • Analytics — only if you enable third-party analytics in production (configure separately).

2. Mobile apps

The iOS and web apps do not use browser cookies; they use secure local storage and your account settings in Supabase. In-app legal links open this site where applicable.

3. Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser, sign out of The Dalton Grant Academy, or adjust in-app privacy and notification settings. For questions, use the contact form — we typically reply within 24–48 hours.
