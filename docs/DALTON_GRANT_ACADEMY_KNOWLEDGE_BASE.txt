1. PRODUCT IDENTITY
================================================================

The Dalton Grant Academy (DGA) is a performance community platform for athletes and creators — built for anyone interested in growth, athletics, or self-improvement. There is no single target user: it welcomes athletes, creators, coaches, scouts, and supporters alike, with a general focus on people aged 13-25+.

AREAS:
- Community: Feed, posts, reactions, comments, direct messages.
- Events: Discover, book, save; hosts create sessions and use QR check-in.
- Media: Training and mindset video/audio library; uploads for verified creators.
- Sponsors: Partner listings and partnership inquiries.
- Profile: Bio, sports, highlights, results, banner, profile photo.
- Grant Access Pro: Paid premium subscription that unlocks creator tools after Dalton verification.

Premium entitlement is named "Grant Access Pro."

PLATFORMS: iOS and Web are live today. Android is NOT yet available (planned, not released).

ACCOUNTS: Supabase Auth. One account works across all your devices — use the same email everywhere, and your subscription status is stored per account.

4. GRANT ACCESS PRO (PREMIUM) & DALTON VERIFICATION
================================================================

Becoming a creator on DGA is a TWO-STEP process.

STEP 1 — SUBSCRIBE TO GRANT ACCESS PRO (PAID):
- Monthly: £11.99
- Annual: £99.99
- No free trial. Billing starts immediately on purchase.
- Where to buy: Web -> Profile -> Premium -> Stripe Checkout. iOS -> RevenueCat paywall (App Store).
- One subscription per account. Don't buy separately on web and mobile for the same email.
- After payment, wait 1-2 minutes, then refresh (web) or use Restore purchases (mobile).

STEP 2 — DALTON VERIFICATION (AUTOMATIC, REQUIRED FOR FEATURES):
Dalton verification is mandatory before a premium account can use its creator features. There is nothing the user needs to submit — verification approval is sent automatically after purchasing Grant Access Pro, usually within 24-48 hours.

While waiting, the user may see "Awaiting Dalton verification." Creator tools stay locked until approval lands. Do not promise instant verification.

IMPORTANT: dalton_verified WITHOUT an active Grant Access Pro subscription does NOT unlock creator tools. Both are required together.

WHAT GRANT ACCESS PRO + DALTON VERIFICATION UNLOCKS:
- Sponsorship collaboration requests
- Media upload requests
- Event creation tools and proposals
- A special premium account badge
- Priority visibility for premium members

Dalton-verified users also tend to see faster response times and proposal decisions, because Dalton knows who he's communicating with.

BILLING, CANCELLATION & REFUNDS:
- No refunds — subscriptions are managed by Apple and Stripe.
- Cancellation is allowed. When you cancel, you keep premium status until what would have been your next billing date, then it ends.
- Cancelling premium only removes premium status. It does NOT remove your dalton_verified status — verification persists.

RESTORE PURCHASES (MOBILE): Profile -> Premium -> Restore purchases, using the same email as the original purchase.

IF THE "+" BUTTON SHOWS A PAYWALL: That means the account isn't premium yet. Subscribe via Web (Stripe) or iOS (App Store).

5. MAIN TABS (DETAILED)
================================================================

COMMUNITY:
- Scroll the feed; pull down to refresh (gold circular loader).
- Create text and image posts where enabled.
- Direct messages and notifications live in the Community area.
- Report content via the in-app report tool.
- Posting limit: Maximum 10 posts per day for ALL users, no exceptions.

Conduct & moderation:
- Mature content = permanent ban. This includes (and is not limited to) sexual or explicit content, nudity, graphic violence or gore, hate speech or discrimination, harassment, and content promoting illegal or dangerous activity. These and similar will not be tolerated.
- Repeated infringements (e.g. swearing, behaviour creating a safety risk) result in a temporary 3-day ban. On review, further sanctions may be added to the account.
- Accounts used in a non-contributive, destructive, demeaning, or hateful manner — including by minors without parental consent — will be permanently closed.

EVENTS:
- Filters: All, Booked, Saved, Past, My events (premium).
- Free and paid events both feature on the platform.
- Create an event (+): requires Grant Access Pro AND Dalton verification.
- Booking: Book an event to attend. Capacity is set by the event host — once an event reaches full capacity, no further applications are accepted.
- No refunds for paid events.
- Cancelling attendance: If you can no longer attend after booking, you must notify the event creator through the app's interface.
- QR check-in: A QR code is generated when you sign up for an event, and the host scans it on the day to check you in.

Your event QR code:
- The QR is permanently attached to your sign-up instance for that event via your account — you can't really "lose" it, because it lives on your account.
- Apple Wallet users can add the QR as a Wallet pass.
- All other users can screenshot the QR, generate a PDF/PNG of it, or simply open the app on the day.
- Strong advice: keep your phone charged and take a screenshot of your QR code as backup — especially if the app isn't displaying your QR correctly, you didn't save a PDF/PNG, and the Apple Wallet pass also isn't working.

MEDIA:
- Browse content by category chips.
- Continue Watching appears for videos you started but didn't finish.
- Bookmark opens your saved library.
- Upload (+): requires Grant Access Pro AND Dalton verification.

Upload rules:
- No maximum video length.
- Accepted formats: .mov, .mp4, .mp3.
- File size: must not exceed a significant size limit — roughly 20GB.
- Visibility: all users can view media content.
- No in-app reporting needed for media — all content is reviewed and checked against content guidelines BEFORE it's published publicly to the Media page.

SPONSORS:
- Browse partner listings.
- Partnership inquiry (+): requires Grant Access Pro; coordinated with the Dalton team.
- How sponsorship works: Interested parties send their proposal and offer for users. Expect a response in roughly 24-48 hours, after which communication continues to develop a fuller understanding of the partnership.

PROFILE & SETTINGS:
- Edit profile sections: basic info, photo, banner, bio, interests, highlights, recent results.
- Settings -> Accessibility: Larger Text (1x-3x), Differentiate Without Color, Sufficient Contrast; dark interface is the default. Preferences sync when signed in.
- Settings -> Legal: Terms, Privacy, Cookie policy, Athlete agreement (website links).
- Settings -> Support / Help centre: in-app and web guides.
